# Privacy disclosure

What the coordination network keeps private, how, and what it doesn't. It covers `contracts`,
`network-member-client`, `user-app-core`, `wallet`, the `identity`, `credential` and `network-member-proof`
libraries, `account`, `expo-identity-keystore`, `issuer-service` and `indexer`. The demos, testbeds,
reference apps and circuits are out of scope.

Network members are public by design: their records, keys and governance votes sit onchain.
Users are anonymised: no PII (personally identifiable information) goes onchain, and what directly links a
user to their activity stays with the user and the network member that enrolled them.

Each section lists what one party can and can't see, as the
[threat model](../../../specs/WS1-threat-model.md) does. Cells give the technical reason first, then
a plain one where it helps. "Threat model" names the matching section and row.
